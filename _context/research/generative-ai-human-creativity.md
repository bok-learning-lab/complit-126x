# Generative Artificial Intelligence, Human Creativity, and Art

**Published:** PNAS Nexus, March 5, 2024
**Authors:** Eric Zhou and Dokyun Lee (Boston University)
**DOI:** 10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae052
**Source:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10914360/

## Abstract

This research examines text-to-image generative AI systems (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, DALL-E) using a dataset of 4+ million artworks from 50,000+ users. Key findings show that AI adoption increased creative output by 25% and improved peer evaluation by 50%. While peak content novelty rose, average novelty declined, indicating "an expanding but inefficient idea space."

## Key Findings

### Creative Productivity
- Upon adoption, artists increased output by 50% in the first month, doubling by the second month
- Roughly 7 additional artworks per month initially, extending to 15 the following month
- Productivity gains persisted beyond adoption

### Creative Value
- Three months post-adoption, AI-generated work received 50% more favorable ratings
- Favorites per view increased from approximately 2% to 3%

### Content Novelty
- Average novelty declined among adopters over time
- Maximum content novelty increased significantly, suggesting "a subset of adopters" exploring new frontiers
- Fine-tuned checkpoints may encourage consistency, reducing diversity

### Visual Novelty
- Both average and maximum visual novelty decreased post-adoption
- Artists gravitated toward preferred visual styles with minimal deviations
- Prompt engineering's formulaic approach may drive "visual homogeneity"

## Individual-Level Analysis

Artists with baseline creative proficiency experienced the greatest success:
- Artists skilled at generating novel ideas saw the strongest value gains
- "Content is king" in text-to-image workflows—conceptual innovation outweighed aesthetic novelty

## "Generative Synesthesia"

The authors propose that successful AI-assisted creativity requires:
1. **Ideation proficiency:** Deconstructing ideas into prompt components
2. **Artistic filtering:** Curating outputs for coherence and quality
3. **Rapid iteration:** Sampling from creative possibility space

### Key Concern
Without continued exploration, the creative ecosystem risks "saturation with generic content" as models train on outdated knowledge.

## Platform-Level Impact

Gini coefficient analysis revealed AI adopters' favorites distributed more evenly than control groups—suggesting "a more evenly distributed" recognition system.

## Implications

"Humans' ideation proficiency and refined artistic filter rather than pure mechanical skill" emerge as critical competencies in AI-assisted creativity. While productivity and peer evaluation improve substantially, aggregate novelty decline threatens long-term creative vitality without deliberate frontier-pushing.
