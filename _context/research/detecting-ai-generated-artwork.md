# Detecting AI-generated Artwork

**arXiv ID:** 2504.07078
**Submitted:** April 9, 2025
**Authors:** Meien Li, Mark Stamp
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07078

## Abstract

The paper addresses the challenge posed by high-quality AI-generated artwork, which has become increasingly difficult to distinguish from human-created art. The researchers evaluated multiple machine learning and deep learning approaches to classify artwork across three artistic styles: baroque, cubism, and expressionism.

## Methods

The models tested included:
- Logistic Regression (LR)
- Support Vector Machine (SVM)
- Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)
- Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)

## Key Findings

- **Multiclass accuracy:** approximately 82% across six classification groups
- **Binary classification** (AI vs. human artwork): nearly 98% accuracy

## Relevance

This research helps frame questions of originality and authorship in Generative AI creative outputs, providing technical classification methods between human vs. AI art.
