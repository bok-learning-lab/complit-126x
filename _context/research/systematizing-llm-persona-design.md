# Systematizing LLM Persona Design: A Four-Quadrant Technical Taxonomy for AI Companion Applications

**Authors:** Esther Sun, Zichu Wu (Carnegie Mellon University)
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/html/2511.02979v1

## Abstract

This paper addresses fragmentation in LLM-based persona design by proposing a unified framework organizing AI companions along two axes: Virtual vs. Embodied deployment and Emotional vs. Functional intent. The resulting four quadrants examine distinct application domains—from virtual romantic companions to embodied care robots—revealing that technical requirements and ethical considerations vary substantially across contexts.

## 1. Introduction

Large Language Models now function as "the core cognitive engines driving complex, personified AI agents" rather than simple text tools. The paper establishes a systematic taxonomy along two primary dimensions:
- **Interaction Intent:** Emotional Connection vs. Functional/Cognitive Augmentation
- **Deployment Modality:** Virtual vs. Embodied Intelligence

## 2. Quadrant I: Virtual Companionship

Virtual companionship manifests in three primary forms:
1. **Interactive Virtual Story Characters** – emergent narratives within simulated environments
2. **Virtual Romantic Companionship** – modeling evolving emotional relationships
3. **Virtual Idols** – multimodal generation for scalable fan engagement

### Four-Layer Technical Analysis Framework

**Model Layer:** Story Characters focus on "deep persona consistency" via RoleLLM; Romantic Companions address "persona drift" through empathy vectors; Virtual Idols pursue "high-fidelity vocal performance."

**Architecture Layer:** Story Characters employ "Generative Agents framework" with "perceive–plan–act" retrieval; Romantic Companions use "stateful RAG architectures"; Virtual Idols leverage "event-driven architecture."

**Generation Layer:** Multi-agent simulation loops, full-duplex spoken dialogue with emotional synthesis, real-time rendering pipelines.

**Safety & Ethics Layer:** Constitutional AI, anti-sycophancy detection, crisis-intervention modules, hybrid moderation frameworks.

## 3. Quadrant II: Functional Virtual Assistants

### Workplace Scenarios
Enterprise applications converge on: enterprise assistants, customer service agents, training simulators. RAG has become central because "the competitive edge of enterprise AI lies not in persona design but in the efficiency and robustness of data pipelines."

### Game Scenarios
Three core challenges: Low-Latency Inference, Believable Emotion and Behavior, Narrative Coherence Dilemma. The gaming industry is becoming "the R&D testbed for the future of embedded AI."

### Mental Health Scenario
**Core Challenges:** The Empathy Paradox (AI lacks genuine affective empathy), Clinical Safety, Regulatory Complexity. The market will split into "Wellness" tier and "Clinical" tier.

## 4. Quadrants III & IV: Persona in Embodied Intelligence

**Quadrant III – General Home Market:** Non-humanoid Companions adopt "pet-like personas"; Functional Assistants employ "utility-first, persona-second" strategies.

**Quadrant IV – Vertical Markets:** Elderly Care featuring "proactive coach" personas; Special Education using "Therapeutic Personas" as "social mediators."

**Core Challenges:** Symbol Grounding Problem, Privacy & Security, Ethics & Legality, Economic Barriers.

## 5. Conclusion

"Persona is not a monolithic concept but a multi-dimensional design space where the core challenges fundamentally change with the application."
