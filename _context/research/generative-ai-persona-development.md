# How Is Generative AI Used for Persona Development?: A Systematic Review of 52 Research Articles

**Authors:** Danial Amin, Joni Salminen, Farhan Ahmed, Sonja M.H. Tervola, Sankalp Sethi, Bernard J. Jansen
**Year:** 2025
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/html/2504.04927v1

## Abstract

This systematic review examines 52 articles (2022-2024) exploring generative AI applications in persona development. Key findings reveal that closed commercial models dominate the field, creating significant market concentration. AI technologies support multiple development stages including data collection, segmentation, enrichment, and evaluation.

## Key Findings

### GenAI Patterns
OpenAI's GPT models appear in approximately 83% of reviewed articles. About 23% of studies employ multiple language models for comparative analysis. Real user data was used in about half of studies; synthetic and mixed datasets comprise roughly 46%.

### Methodologies
Mixed methods approaches dominate at 52%, compared to quantitative (29%) and qualitative (19%). Only 19% follow standard persona development pipelines.

### Applications
HCI education and training represent the most frequent use case at 19%, followed by product development (15%) and accessibility improvements (12%).

### Evaluation Methods
Approximately 44% of articles lack explicit evaluation methodologies. Only 23% employ LLMs for evaluation; 33% rely on human evaluators.

### Ethical Considerations
About 69% of articles explicitly discuss ethical dimensions. Four primary concerns:
1. **Bias and Representation:** Risk of perpetuating stereotypes
2. **Societal Harms:** Creating deceptive entities or amplifying polarization
3. **Trust Issues:** Persona authenticity and reliability
4. **Research Ethics:** Transparency, validation, and disclosure protocols

### Human-AI Collaboration
Approximately 35% incorporate participatory approaches, yet most lack defined protocols for human oversight.

## Critical Limitations
- Demographic representation proved difficult, particularly for marginalized groups
- Statistical alignment between generated and actual population characteristics diverged significantly
- Language bias favored English-language resources

## Future Research Directions
Expanding demographic diversity, developing improved prompt engineering strategies, establishing standardized validation frameworks, conducting longitudinal studies.
